Source. ESPN's public cricket feed, the same JSON their own site reads. No key, no vendor.
Refresh. A GitHub Action re-syncs four times a day and republishes this page, so it stays current whether or not any laptop is switched on.
Amber marks India fixtures and ICC world tournaments — on Polymarket those two draw multiples of everything else, which is why they get their own colour rather than a footnote. The IPL and the WPL count as India here even though no team is called India.
Hollow bars are projections, not fixtures. A season ESPN has not published yet — the IPL spends most of the year in that state — is drawn from the wallchart in The Cricket Window (28 Aug 2026) with its expected match count, and is replaced by real fixtures the moment the feed has them. Nothing beyond the horizon is projected at all: that far out a guess is worth less than an honest gap.
Minor cricket is hidden by default — second-XI, provincial, A-team and age-group fixtures, which the feed returns in bulk. The toggle above brings them back.
